# Reading notes — COA PDFs

41 PDFs read, 50 result lines in total. Text PDFs were read from their text layer; the 10 scans have no text layer and were read visually from the embedded page images (rotated where needed).

Conversions applied: Meridian dates are already YYYY-MM-DD and amounts are in mg. Coastal dates are MM/DD/YYYY and amounts are in mg; purity is on page 1 and mass on page 2. Northgate dates are DD.MM.YYYY, amounts are in grams with a decimal comma, and masses use a decimal comma.

Ambiguous dates: several dates could be read either way (for example CBA 04/03/2026, 06/11/2026, 11/06/2025, 12/05/2025, 01/09/2026, 03/04/2025, and NPT 02.08.2026, 08.02.2026, 12.05.2026). Each one was resolved using the format printed on that lab's own report: Coastal says "All dates on this report are MM/DD/YYYY" and Northgate says "Dates DD.MM.YYYY". Meridian dates are already ISO.
